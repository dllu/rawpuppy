# 2026-10-09 05:26 — Moebius inference-mask comparison

The recent-model comparison had tested inference-mask growth for FLUX but not
for native Moebius. The Rust benchmark now accepts optional `--mask-padding`,
retains the expanded mask and inferred composition, and commits only through the
original selection. Zero padding preserves its previous behavior. The editor's
sampling defaults are unchanged.

The public removal and withheld actual-pixel GFX fabric patch were each run with
zero and 16-pixel growth, twice at seed 42, FP32, 20 steps and strength 1.0. All
repeated outputs were identical, and every unselected final RGB byte was preserved.
The original fabric output hash also matches the previous native run. Independent
Pillow binary dilation matches the masks generated by the benchmark.

The expanded removal continues the wall and vegetation more plausibly than this
unpadded Moebius run, but the original selection still leaves fringes, seams and
shadows when composited. Fabric reconstruction changes little: mean absolute
error is 4.634 without growth and 4.645 with growth on the 8-bit channel scale.
Warm native inference is about 2.96–2.97 seconds in this shared GB10 session.
These two inputs do not establish a general model winner or a new default.

The public mask contains fractional edge pixels; the Moebius and FLUX adapters
have different internal mask and final color-composition conventions. Those
limits, settings, hashes and measurements are recorded in
[the comparison](inpainting-comparison.md) and
[the data record](data/moebius-halo-gb10-2026-10-09.json).
